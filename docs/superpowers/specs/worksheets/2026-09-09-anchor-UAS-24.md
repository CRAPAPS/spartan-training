## What this document is for

Spartan Training is replacing its 24-hour lockout. Today, if a learner gets a **critical**
exam question wrong, they are locked out of that assessment for a full day. That is being
removed. In its place, the learner must go back and re-read the specific slide that teaches
the point they got wrong, then write a short corrective action. As soon as they do, the
assessment reopens — there is no waiting period at all.

For that to work, **every critical question needs to know which slide teaches its point.**

**Your task:** for each DECISION below, read the slides, read the exam question, and write
the slide id a learner should be sent back to re-read. Everything you need is in this
document. No system access is required.

### You are NOT limited to the question's own module

Capstone and final-exam questions test material taught in earlier modules — that is what a
capstone is. So each module section gives you **two** things:

1. **Every slide in the question's own module, in full text**
2. **An index of every earlier slide in the same track** — id and heading, one line each

Pick from either. If the right slide is in the index and you want its full text before
committing, say so and it will be sent.

Only slides from *earlier or the same* module are offered: the learner has already passed
those, so the link always works. They cannot be sent forward into locked content.

### How to answer

- Write the slide id, e.g. `PI-06-s02`
- Write `NONE` only if nothing in the eligible list teaches the point
- Pick the slide that **teaches** the point, not a summary or an overview that mentions it
- If two slides both cover it, pick the one that explains *why*, not the one that just states the rule

#### UAS-24-s01

**What Georgia Law Requires of You — A Final Review**

Before you begin the examination, review the legal framework that governs every action you take as a licensed unarmed security officer in Georgia.

AUTHORITY:
• You are not a peace officer — you are an authorized agent of a private property owner
• Your arrest authority: felony in your presence only (OCGA § 17-4-60)
• Your detention authority: none — you may request a person remain; you may not compel it for misdemeanors

USE OF FORCE:
• Only proportional to the threat you face
• Only when necessary to protect yourself or others from imminent bodily harm
• Documented immediately after any use

SEARCH & SEIZURE:
• Consensual searches only — refusal ends your authority
• No warrantless searches of persons or property without consent

REPORTING:
• Every incident reported contemporaneously in objective language
• No alteration of reports after the fact

LICENSING:
• GBPDSA license current and on your person during every shift
• Renewal obligations met — lapsed license = working without authority

ETHICS:
• Prohibited conduct: bribery, unauthorized disclosure, use of position for personal gain
• Mandatory reporting: witnessed misconduct by fellow officers

Key points:
  - Arrest authority: felony in presence only — OCGA § 17-4-60
  - Use of force: proportional, necessary, documented
  - Searches: consensual only — refusal ends your authority
  - GBPDSA license: on person every shift, current, renewal obligations met

Legal reference: OCGA § 17-4-60; OCGA § 43-38-10; OCGA § 16-3-21; GBPDSA Code of Conduct; GA Admin Code 509-3-.01

#### UAS-24-s02

**Critical Questions — What They Test & Why**

Three questions in this examination are designated as critical. A wrong answer on any critical question triggers a mandatory 24-hour review period before you may re-attempt the examination.

Critical questions test three areas where mistakes cause irreversible harm:

1. USE OF FORCE LIMITS: Misunderstanding your force authority can result in criminal charges, civil liability, and serious injury. The standard must be second nature.

2. ARREST AUTHORITY: Performing an unlawful arrest or detention violates the subject's constitutional rights and exposes you and your employer to false imprisonment claims under OCGA § 51-7-20.

3. EMERGENCY PRIORITY: Responding to a major emergency in the wrong priority order — evidence before life safety, for instance — can cost a life. The life-safety-first principle is non-negotiable.

If you are required to review before re-attempting: use the 24 hours. Return to the modules covering your missed areas. The examination is not punitive — it ensures that anyone who holds this certificate has genuinely mastered the material that keeps both the public and the officer safe.

Good luck. The State of Georgia, your employer, and the public you protect are counting on you to know this material.

Key points:
  - Three critical questions: use of force, arrest authority, emergency priority order
  - Wrong critical answer = 24-hour mandatory review period, not permanent disqualification
  - Use the review period — re-read the relevant modules before re-attempting
  - Mastery of these three areas protects you, the public, and your employer

Legal reference: GA Admin Code 509-3-.01; OCGA § 51-7-20; OCGA § 16-3-21; OCGA § 17-4-60
